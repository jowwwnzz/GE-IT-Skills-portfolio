I am an ambitious creator and strategist who thrives at the intersection of high-stakes business and complex logistics. ## Social Media Bundle Showcase

### Asset 1: LinkedIn Profile Banner


**Design Reflection:**  
For the LinkedIn banner, I chose a clean, black background to make it easy to the eyes and white accents, texts, and highlights to create a meaningful contrast without making your eyes squint. This design choice reflects my personality as someone who enjoys working in the dark and my enjoyment for practical simplicity and minimalism.

### Asset 2: Square Promotional Post


**Design Reflection:**  
The square promo was structured to instantly grab attention on a fast-moving social media feed by utilizing a high-contrast split layout. Placing a striking image of the Airbus A380 at the top immediately hooks aviation enthusiasts, while the lower text section uses bold, clear typography to introduce the business angle.
